* Differences from SPEC section 5 (for a decision)
- The guide has an Introduction and a Publishing chapter. The spec's scope table has neither; section 14 of the spec puts Publishing out until after phase 10.
- The guide's Exporting chapter names ASCII/UTF-8, HTML, LaTeX and iCalendar only. The spec also lists Markdown and OpenDocument. Under the spec's own rule (scope is the guide) these are extras.
- The guide has no separate chapter for Babel languages beyond "Working with Source Code"; Lisp and JavaScript come from the spec, not the guide.
