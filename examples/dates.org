#+TITLE: Dates, repeaters and deadlines
# CC0 1.0: public domain. Put the cursor on a timestamp and press S-up or S-down; C-c C-s and C-c C-d set a date.

* TODO Pay the rent
SCHEDULED: <2026-12-01 Tue +1m>
Marking it DONE moves the date a month on and keeps the task open.
* TODO Water the plants
SCHEDULED: <2026-11-24 Tue 08:00 +1w>
* TODO File the report
DEADLINE: <2026-12-04 Fri -3d>
The -3d warns three days ahead in the agenda.
* A meeting
<2026-11-25 Wed 14:00-15:30>
An active timestamp shows in the agenda; an inactive one, [2026-11-25 Wed], stays in the text.
