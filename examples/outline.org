* Projects
Headings start with stars; the more stars, the deeper the heading. TAB on a heading folds and unfolds it.
** Garden
Move a heading up or down with M-up and M-down; promote and demote with M-left and M-right.

* Notes
C-c C-x C-w cuts the subtree at the cursor, C-c C-x M-w copies it, and C-c C-x C-y pastes it as a subtree. Cut this whole Notes heading, move to the start of Projects, and paste: Notes goes before it. C-u 2 before C-w takes two siblings.
