* Projects
Headings start with stars; the more stars, the deeper the heading. TAB on a heading folds and unfolds it.

* Notes
C-c C-x C-w cuts the subtree at the cursor, C-c C-x M-w copies it, and C-c C-x C-y pastes it as a subtree. Cut this whole Notes heading, move to the start of Projects, and paste: Notes goes before it. C-u 2 before C-w takes two siblings. In the Outline view, tap a heading and use Cut subtree, Copy subtree and Paste subtree after.

* Lists
Put the cursor on an item and try the keys. M-RET adds an item, M-S-RET one with a checkbox, and C-c - cycles the bullet. M-up and M-down move an item, M-right and M-left change its level, and C-c ^ sorts the list.

* Notes and logging
C-c C-z on this heading asks for a note and files it under the heading as "Note taken on [time]". With org-log-done set to note in the settings file, finishing a TODO adds CLOSED: and a closing note.
