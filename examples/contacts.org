#+TITLE: Contacts and birthdays
# CC0 1.0: public domain. Settings, Emacs, Contacts files names this file (for example github:contacts.org); the diary example and the agenda-birthdays example then show these birthdays.

A birthday is a date such as 1990-11-23, an Org timestamp such as <1990-11-23 Fri>, or 11-23 without a year.
* Jane Doe
:PROPERTIES:
:EMAIL: jane.doe@example.com
:BIRTHDAY: 1990-11-23
:END:
* John Smith
:PROPERTIES:
:EMAIL: john.smith@example.com
:BIRTHDAY: 11-23
:END:
