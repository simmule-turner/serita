#+TITLE: Contacts and birthdays
# CC0 1.0: public domain. Settings, Emacs, Contacts files names this file (for example github:contacts.org); the diary example and the agenda-birthdays example then show these birthdays.

A birthday is a date such as 1990-11-23, an Org timestamp such as <1990-11-23 Fri>, or 11-23 without a year.
Jane and John use the flat layout of org-vcard; Ada uses its tree layout, where a heading with FIELDTYPE birthday holds the date.
* Jane Doe
:PROPERTIES:
:EMAIL: jane.doe@example.com
:BIRTHDAY: 1990-11-23
:END:
* John Smith
:PROPERTIES:
:EMAIL: john.smith@example.com
:BIRTHDAY: 11-23
:END:
* Ada Lovelace
:PROPERTIES:
:KIND: individual
:FIELDTYPE: name
:END:
** 1815-12-10
:PROPERTIES:
:FIELDTYPE: birthday
:END:
** ada@analytical-labs.example.org
:PROPERTIES:
:FIELDTYPE: email-work
:END:
